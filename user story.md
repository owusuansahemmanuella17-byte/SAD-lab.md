User Story: Workplace Team Project

As a team member,
I want to communicate and collaborate with my colleagues when completing a project,
so that we can finish the project on time and satisfy the client.

Gherkin Acceptance Criteria

Scenario: Successfully completing a team project

Given the manager has assigned tasks to each team member
And each team member understands their responsibility

When a team member encounters a problem
And they communicate the problem to the team
And the team works together to solve the problem

Then the team should complete all assigned tasks
And review the final project
And present the completed project to the client
And successfully meet the project requirements.
